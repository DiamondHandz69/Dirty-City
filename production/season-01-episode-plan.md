Status: story outline, NOT rendered video. Target: 15 minutes per episode, weekly releases after token launch. No release dates promised.

00:30–01:30 TITLE + AFTERMATH — Sirens, neon streets, fragmented emergency broadcasts; establish a mysterious holiday shipment vanished.
01:30–03:15 DIRTY — Boss learns the shipment's disappearance was a setup; decides to assemble a crew before the city locks down.
03:15–04:45 LA JEFA — Driving sequence; she extracts a contact from a checkpoint and warns DIRTY the routes are compromised.
04:45–06:15 DREAD — Quiet infiltration; he finds surveillance pointing to an insider.

07:45–09:00 SHEIKH — Hacker decodes a partial map and reveals a time limit; visual screens must be legible and fictional.
09:00–10:15 CONNECT — Deal broker secures access but demands a favor that will matter in Episode 2.

11:30–12:45 JOBBER — Establish his role in the operation based on approved character dossier; avoid inventing an unapproved biography.
12:45–13:45 THE NINTH DOSSIER — Hold for verified ninth approved character, with a silent silhouette/closed file until identity is confirmed.
13:45–15:00 THE CREW ASSEMBLES — Nine dossiers on table; mission objective revealed; cliffhanger: an unknown watcher already knows the plan. END CARD: “TO BE CONTINUED.”

## Asset and production pipeline (free-first)
1. Verify the original approved nine-character poster and dossiers.
2. Export each portrait as a labeled character-reference sheet. Do not generate face variants without review.
3. Prepare voice-over/dialogue and an animatic using stills, pans, zooms, title cards and existing clips.
4. Generate a small number of character-anchored action clips using available free video credits; reject inconsistent faces.
5. Assemble and render a 15-minute rough cut; mark still-image sequences honestly.
6. Add a native HTML5 video player to the existing website only after the actual movie media is hosted and tested.
7. Repeat weekly as capacity allows; no claim of automatic unattended generation or guaranteed release cadence.
