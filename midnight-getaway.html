Explore the city.

On phones: use the left thumbstick to move or steer, the right thumb to interact, run or jump. In a car, hold GAS or BRAKE and steer with the stick. Drag the open game view to look around; the gear menu contains camera, graphics and weather options. On desktop: WASD or arrows, SHIFT for boost, J to jump, E to interact. Space pauses. Switch to first person for an immersive street-level view. Toggle rain or night. Use Q or the special button for your crew ability. Earn local XP and medals to unlock missions. Character models remain procedural approximations; custom rigged character meshes are not included.
