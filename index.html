THE ORIGIN // OUR LORE

EVERY CITY HAS A DIRTY SIDE.

Nine outsiders. One city built on money, power and betrayal.

Welcome to DIRTY CITY, a fictional neon-lit underworld where loyalty is rare, ambition runs wild and everyone has a price. Dirty, the Boss from Colombia, assembles an international crew of nine specialists. Each arrives with a past, a motive and a different idea of loyalty.

Season One begins during the holidays. A mysterious Christmas shipment brings the crew together, but missing cargo and fractured alliances threaten to tear them apart. The Christmas Heist is only the beginning of a story about trust, survival and the price of power.

$DIRTY is the planned Solana token connected to our original story-driven meme project. The universe is built to expand through character stories, cinematic episodes, community events and a planned browser-based game.
